Emergency NTC-1188
Water supply shutdown — Tower B, 16 August
Posted12 Aug 2026, 18:40
AuthorS. Krishnan · Society Admin
Applies toTower B · all flats
NOTICE ATTACHMENT · RISER PLAN · DROP IMAGE HERE
The main riser feeding flats 101–1204 in Tower B will be replaced on Saturday, 16 August. Supply will be closed from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Please store drinking and utility water the night before. Two tankers will be stationed near Gate 2 from noon; security will manage queueing per floor. Lifts stay operational throughout. If supply hasn't returned by 5 PM, raise a Plumbing complaint from the app and the on-duty supervisor will be paged.
Emergency notices are delivered to every resident regardless of notification preferences and are recorded in the society audit log.
